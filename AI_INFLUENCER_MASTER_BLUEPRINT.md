Traditional "aesthetic" influencers (like 2018–2020 Lil Miquela) relied on lifestyle flexing and likes. The algorithm prioritizes:
1. **Watch Time & Retention** (hooks that stop scrolling within 0.5s)
2. **Comment Volume** (driving conversational loops)
3. **Shares & Saves** (saving recipes, hacks, product recommendations)

* **The Result:** 16M+ views per reel, 40,000+ comments, 800k+ followers in ~300 posts, and **$240,000+** in affiliate/e-commerce sales.

## 🎬 Phase-by-Phase Execution Guide

### Phase 1: Character Creation (Higgsfield AI Influencer Studio)
1. Go to **Higgsfield AI** ➔ Select **AI Influencer Studio** ➔ **Create New**.
2. **Prompt for Base Character**:
   > *"A friendly older Asian woman with dark hair and dark eyes wearing an orange yoga outfit. She should look very healthy and vibrant, and she should be fit for her age. She should look about 50 years old, but be in very good shape. She is pretty and natural. Photorealistic, 8k skin texture."*
3. **Refine/Edit Character**:
   * If hair is tied up: *"Please have her hair down and make her a little bit older, like seven or eight years older."*
4. Save & download the master portrait to lock facial identity.

### Phase 2: Scene Composition & The "Microphone Interview" Look
The "street interview" or "beach interview" format feels organic, unscripted, and candid—tricking viewers into pausing rather than skipping an ad.

1. In Higgsfield, open your character inside **Nano Banana Pro** (or image editor).
2. Set aspect ratio to **9:16** (Reels/TikTok standard) and resolution to **2K or 4K**.
3. **Scene Composite Prompt**:
   > *"Have her standing at the beach in Tulum at sunset with warm golden hour lighting. There should be someone's hand holding a wireless reporter microphone on the right side of the screen like she is being interviewed. She should be looking slightly at the interviewer off-camera, not directly at the camera lens. Hyperrealistic, natural skin pores, neck veins, subtle flyaway hair blowing in ocean breeze, palm trees soft blurred bokeh in the background."*
4. Generate 4 options, pick the frame where lighting hits the face naturally and posture matches an interview.

### Phase 3: Video Animation & Dialogue (Kling 3.0)
1. In Higgsfield, navigate to **Video** ➔ Select **Kling 3.0**.
2. Upload your generated image as the **Start Frame**.
3. **Crucial Settings**:
   * **Multi-shot:** `OFF` (Keeps the camera locked on the interview single-take).
   * **Model:** `Kling 3.0`.
   * **Duration:** `10 seconds`.
4. **Dialogue / Motion Prompt**:
   > *"Please have her speaking into the microphone like she is being interviewed, naturally gesturing with slight head tilt and eye contact with the interviewer, saying: 'Most people think I'm 50, but really I'm 239 years old. I look this good because of one simple ancient secret. Comment AGE and I'll send you the exact recipe.'"*
5. Click **Generate** and download the resulting 1080x1920 clip.

### Phase 4: Editing for Maximum Algorithm Retention (CapCut)
1. **Pacing & Trimming:** Trim dead air at 0.0s so the speech starts immediately on frame 1.
2. **Viral Captions:** 
   * Add dynamic animated captions (Word-by-word animation, Bold font, Yellow/White/Green highlights).
   * Emphasize trigger words: `50`, `239 YEARS OLD`, `SECRET`, `COMMENT AGE`.
3. **Audio Layering (The Secret to Realism):**
   * *Track 1:* Vocal audio from Kling / ElevenLabs.
   * *Track 2:* Low ambient wind & ocean wave background sound (15% volume) to mask AI stillness.
   * *Track 3:* Subtle trending low-fi beat or upbeat acoustic guitar (10% volume).
4. **Micro-Motion:** Add a slow 102% keyframe push-in (slow zoom) across the 10 seconds to hold visual attention.

### Phase 5: The ManyChat Comment-to-Cash Funnel
1. Connect **ManyChat** to your Instagram Professional/Creator account.
2. Create an **Instagram Automation**:
   * **Trigger:** User comments on your Reel containing keywords: `AGE`, `SECRET`, `YES`, `RECIPE`.
   * **Action 1 (Public Comment Reply):** ManyChat automatically replies to their comment with randomized friendly replies:
     * *"Just sent the secret to your DMs darling! Check your inbox 🌿"*
     * *"Sent your way! Hope it helps your wellness journey ❤️"*
     *(This skyrockets the comment count into the algorithm!)*
   * **Action 2 (Direct Message):**
     > *"Hello dear! Thank you for watching my video 🌺*
     > 
     > *The ancient daily routine I use is Jamaican Soursop Bitters & wildcrafted Moringa leaf extract every morning on an empty stomach.*
     > 
     > *Here is the exact clean batch I order from Amazon: [YOUR_AMAZON_AFFILIATE_LINK]*
     > 
     > *Let me know if you try it! Wishing you endless youth & health."*
3. **Revenue Model**:
   * Product: Soursop Bitters ($35.99 on Amazon).
   * Amazon Affiliate commission (10% on health/beauty + halo sales on their whole cart) OR TikTok Shop / Private Label Dropshipping ($20 margin per bottle).
   * 40,000 comments ➔ ~30,000 DMs delivered ➔ ~7,500 link clicks ➔ ~1,100 orders = **$15,000–$25,000+ per viral video**.
