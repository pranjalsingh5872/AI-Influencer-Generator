## ⚙️ 2. Step-by-Step ManyChat Setup Guide

### Step 1: Connect Instagram Professional Account
1. Ensure your Instagram account is switched to **Creator** or **Business** account (Settings ➔ Account Type).
2. Connect your Instagram account to a Facebook Page.
3. Log into **[ManyChat.com](https://manychat.com)** ➔ Click **Connect New Channel** ➔ Select **Instagram**.
4. In Instagram App: Go to **Settings ➔ Messages and story replies ➔ Message controls ➔ Allow access to messages** (Toggle ON).

---

### Step 2: Create the "Comment-to-DM" Growth Tool
1. In ManyChat, go to **Automation ➔ New Flow**.
2. Name the flow: `Viral Reel - Soursop Bitters DM Funnel`.
3. Click **Add Trigger** ➔ Select **User comments on your Post or Reel**.
4. **Trigger Configuration**:
   * **Which post?** Select *"Any Post or Reel"* OR specify the exact video URL.
   * **Comments contain specific keywords?** Select *"Comments contain any of these keywords"*.
   * **Keywords:** Add: `SECRET`, `secret`, `Secret`, `AGE`, `age`, `YES`, `yes`, `RECIPE`, `recipe`.
   * **Exclude keywords:** (Optional) spam filters like `bot`, `fake`, `promo`.

---

### Step 3: Configure Auto-Response to Public Comments
*Under the Trigger settings in ManyChat, turn on **Auto-reply to comments**:*
* Add 4 to 6 randomized responses so Instagram doesn't flag repetitive spam:
  1. *"Sent to your DMs, my dear! Check your message requests 🌿"*
  2. *"Just sent the recipe to your inbox darling! Hope you love it ❤️"*
  3. *"Check your DMs! Wishing you endless health & vitality ✨"*
  4. *"Sent it over! Make sure to take it every morning before coffee 🌺"*
  5. *"In your inbox now sweet child! Let me know how it works for you 🙏"*
